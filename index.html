01 / Diagnose the opportunity

The mobile problem is
at the decision point.

Funnel data narrowed the opportunity. Qualitative review then suggested a mechanism: people were comparing too much information before they understood the difference between covers.

Funnel conversion by device

The largest device gap appears at cover selection.

Observed friction

31%

of mobile sessions reaching cover selection abandon without opening a cover detail.

Evidence triangulation

  • Scroll depth falls after the third pricing block
  • “What’s included?” is the top help query
  • Error rate stays stable—this is not a form problem

02 / Design a test that can answer the question

Reduce comparison effort,
not customer choice.

Pre-registered hypothesis

For mobile quote visitors, a recommended cover plus a concise comparison will improve quote completion by at least 1.5pp, without a meaningful increase in support intent or time-to-complete.

Population
Mobile quote visitors, DK
Allocation
50 / 50, persistent visitor assignment
Primary metric

04 / Make the result dependable

Quality is part of the experiment.

01

Pre-register

Metric definitions, exclusions, minimum sample, and decision criteria were set before exposure.

02

Validate

Browser/device QA, event validation, assignment consistency, and a sample-ratio mismatch check ran before reading results.

03

Decide responsibly

Conversion is balanced with guardrails. Roll out in stages, retain a holdout, and monitor downstream outcomes.
